Her Story

From Wheelchair to Thriving

Dr. V is a medical doctor who built her career through sheer determination — growing up in poverty, walking miles to sell goods, and putting herself through school to fulfill her dream of healing others.

In the late 1990s, a simple bus accident changed everything. Her L5 lumbar vertebra shattered out of place, leaving her in incapacitating pain. Two surgeries failed. Bone fragments damaged her spinal cord. Doctors told her she would remain in a pain-ridden, weakened state permanently.

Bedridden and in despair, using a wheelchair, she discovered an alternative path — one her own son Eliot introduced her to: meditation, visualization, and gratitude. Slowly, she rewired her mind. The pain crises became less frequent. Her conviction grew. And she stood up.

Today Dr. V practices medicine, speaks internationally, and shares her Three Pillars of Self-Healing with anyone willing to believe that recovery is possible.
